Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Prayer Hour Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Daily Prayer Hour to upload and manage videos on that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, the app uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own channel, and updates titles, descriptions, tags and playlists of those videos. It does not read, store or process data belonging to any other user.

What is stored

An OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer so uploads can run. No personal data is collected, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
